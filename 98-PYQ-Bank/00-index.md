## 2. Honest note on years

- The exam and year tags come from memory-based compilations of past papers (APPSC AEE, SSC JE Mechanical, RRB JE). They could not be checked against the official question papers while preparing this repository.
- Several questions are asked repeatedly across years and shifts, so a tag shows one occurrence, not the only one.
- Every row has a `Verify` box. Tick it only after you confirm the question and year in an official paper or a trusted compiled source.
- The answer to each question is derived from standard theory and is reliable regardless of the tag. Treat the year as a guide to the exam pattern, not as a citation.

## 3. How to practise

- Attempt a full set in a timed window (about 1 minute per question).
- Cover the answer column first. Log every miss in [`../99-Revision-System/03-error-log-template.md`](../99-Revision-System/03-error-log-template.md).
- Return to the chapter file for each missed concept.
