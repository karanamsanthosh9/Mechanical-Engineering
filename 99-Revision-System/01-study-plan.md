# 01. Study Plan

A phased plan for APPSC AEE (Mechanical), SSC JE Mechanical and RRB JE Mechanical. All three papers are objective (MCQ); the plan below is built around reading each chapter once, solving numericals, then revising by checklist and mock practice.

---

## 1. How to use the repository

- **Read** one chapter file completely.
- **Solve** the worked numericals without looking at the answer, then attempt the chapter PYQ table.
- **Tick** the revision checklist at the end of the chapter only when you can answer each item from memory.
- **Log** every wrong answer in [`03-error-log-template.md`](03-error-log-template.md).
- **Verify** PYQ years: the `Verify` column is for you to check against official papers. Tick it once confirmed.

## 2. Priority order (by exam weight)

| Priority | Subjects | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Thermodynamics, Heat Transfer, IC Engines, RAC, Steam and Power Plants | Largest combined share in all three exams |
| 2 | Fluid Mechanics, Fluid Machinery | High share; formula-driven and quick to score |
| 3 | Strength of Materials, Theory of Machines, Machine Design | Numericals; core for APPSC AEE |
| 4 | Manufacturing Processes, Material Science | Theory-heavy; very high return in SSC JE and RRB JE |
| 5 | Industrial Engineering, Engineering Mechanics | Short, scoring topics |

Always confirm the current syllabus and marks distribution from the latest official notification of each exam, because they change between cycles.

## 4. Daily routine (about 6 hours)

| Block | Time | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| Learn | 2 h | New chapter reading with notes |
| Numericals | 1.5 h | Worked numericals and chapter PYQs |
| Revision | 1 h | Previous day's chapter and the formula handbook |
| Practice | 1 h | Timed MCQs |
| Error review | 0.5 h | Update the error log |

## 5. Exam-specific focus

| Exam | Emphasis |
|---|---|
| **APPSC AEE** | Deeper concept and numerical MCQs across the full syllabus, including SOM, TOM, Machine Design and Thermal; also the general studies section of the notification |
| **SSC JE Mechanical** | Direct formula-based and conceptual MCQs; Paper 1 includes general intelligence and general awareness along with the technical section; Paper 2 is technical only |
| **RRB JE** | Wide, moderately easy MCQs; fast revision of definitions and standard values; the technical section is combined with general subjects in CBT 2 |

Check the latest notification for the exact paper pattern, negative marking and syllabus.

## 6. Revision cycles

- **Cycle 1 (end of each week):** revise that week's chapters using only the checklists.
- **Cycle 2 (weeks 13 to 14):** read the master formula handbook daily; solve the PYQ bank by subject.
- **Cycle 3 (final 10 days):** use [`04-last-week-revision-sheet.md`](04-last-week-revision-sheet.md), full-length mocks, and the error log.

## 7. Test strategy

- Attempt easy and formula-direct questions first.
- Use the unit-check habit: confirm the units of the result match the quantity asked.
- Keep a list of standard values (critical ratios, efficiencies, constants) from the handbook.
- Review negative marking policy before deciding on guessing.
