# 04. Last-Week Revision Sheet

Use only in the final 7 to 10 days. Each line is a fact that is commonly tested as a direct MCQ. Do not learn new topics now; fix gaps from the error log.

---

## 1. Thermodynamics and Heat Transfer

- Otto efficiency depends on $r$ and $\gamma$ only; for the same $r$, Otto > Diesel; for the same peak pressure, Diesel > Otto.
- Brayton efficiency depends on pressure ratio only.
- Carnot efficiency uses absolute temperatures; no real engine beats it.
- Throttling is isenthalpic; for an ideal gas the temperature does not change.
- Entropy of an isolated system never decreases.
- COP of heat pump = COP of refrigerator + 1.
- Critical radius of insulation: cylinder $k/h$, sphere $2k/h$.
- Lumped analysis valid for $\text{Bi} < 0.1$.
- Fin efficiency falls as fin length increases.
- Counter-flow exchanger is more effective than parallel flow for the same $UA$.
- Wien: $\lambda_{max}T = 2898\ \mu\text{m K}$; radiation $\propto T^4$.

## 2. Thermal machines

- Four-stroke: one power stroke per two revolutions; camshaft at half the crank speed.
- Octane number = antiknock quality of petrol; cetane number = ignition quality of diesel (high cetane = short delay).
- SI knock is end-gas auto-ignition; diesel knock is a long ignition delay.
- Morse test: multi-cylinder SI engines; Willans line: CI engines.
- Critical pressure ratio: $0.528$ air, $0.546$ superheated steam, $0.577$ dry saturated steam.
- Impulse turbine $R = 0$; Parsons $R = 0.5$.
- Optimum speed ratio: single-stage impulse $\dfrac{\cos\alpha}{2}$; Parsons $\cos\alpha$.
- Minimum compressor work: isothermal; two-stage intermediate pressure $\sqrt{p_1p_3}$; clearance does not change work per kg.
- Surface condenser recovers condensate; jet condenser does not.
- Mountings: safety valve, water level indicator, pressure gauge, stop valve, blow-off cock, fusible plug. Accessories: economiser, superheater, air preheater, feed pump.
- 1 TR = 3.5167 kW; expansion valve is isenthalpic; R-134a is an HFC with zero ODP.
- In sensible heating, specific humidity is constant; saturated air has DBT = WBT = DPT.
- Load factor = average / maximum demand; diversity factor $\ge 1$.

## 3. Fluids

- Viscosity of liquids falls with temperature; of gases rises.
- Centre of pressure lies below the centroid.
- Laminar pipe flow: $\text{Re} < 2000$, $f = 64/\text{Re}$, $u_{max} = 2V_{avg}$.
- Maximum power through a pipe at $h_f = H/3$.
- Best rectangular channel: $b = 2y$. Critical flow $\text{Fr} = 1$.
- Reynolds law for pipe flow models; Froude law for free-surface flows.
- Pelton: high head, impulse, no draft tube. Francis: medium head. Kaplan: low head, adjustable blades.
- Specific speed: Pelton low, Francis medium, Kaplan high.
- Centrifugal pump: backward-curved vanes, start with the delivery valve closed, NPSH available > required.
- Fluid coupling does not multiply torque; torque converter does.

## 4. Solid mechanics and machines

- $E = 2G(1+\nu) = 3K(1-2\nu)$; $\nu$ for steel $\approx 0.3$.
- Maximum shear stress in a rectangle $= 1.5\times$ average; in a circle $= \tfrac43\times$ average.
- Euler load ratios (hinged : fixed-free : fixed-fixed : fixed-hinged) $= 1 : \tfrac14 : 4 : 2$.
- Thin cylinder: hoop stress $=$ twice the longitudinal stress.
- Torsion: $\dfrac{T}{J} = \dfrac{\tau}{r} = \dfrac{G\theta}{L}$; hollow shaft is stronger per unit weight.
- Grubler: $F = 3(n-1) - 2j - h$; Grashof: $s + l \le p + q$.
- Interference is avoided by increasing teeth or pressure angle, or by using stub teeth or profile correction.
- Flywheel stores energy, governor controls mean speed.
- Transmissibility below 1 only above $r = \sqrt2$.
- Goodman and Soderberg lines in fatigue; endurance limit about $0.5\,\sigma_u$ for steel.
- Ball bearing life exponent $3$; roller bearing $\dfrac{10}{3}$.

## 5. Manufacturing

- Chvorinov: $t_s \propto (V/A)^2$; the riser must freeze last.
- Neutral flame for mild steel; oxidising for brass; carburising for hard facing.
- Straight polarity (electrode negative) gives deeper penetration at the work.
- Blanking: clearance on the punch; punching: clearance on the die.
- Hot working above the recrystallisation temperature ($\approx 0.4\,T_m$).
- Taylor: $VT^n = C$; $n$ increases from HSS to carbide to ceramic.
- Merchant: $2\phi + \beta - \alpha = 90^\circ$.
- Up milling: chip thickness starts at zero; down milling needs backlash elimination.
- Grinding: hard material gets a soft grade wheel.
- Go gauge checks maximum material; hole basis system: H hole.
- G00 rapid, G01 linear, G02/G03 circular, G90 absolute, M03 spindle on clockwise, M30 end of program.
- USM for hard brittle materials; EDM and ECM for conductive hard materials.

## 6. Material science

- APF: BCC $0.68$, FCC and HCP $0.74$. Atoms per cell: BCC 2, FCC 4, HCP 6.
- Fe-C: eutectoid $0.77\%$ at $727^\circ$C; eutectic $4.3\%$ at $1147^\circ$C; max C in austenite $2.14\%$; cementite $6.67\%$.
- Pearlite = ferrite + cementite; ledeburite = austenite + cementite; martensite = BCT supersaturated solid solution.
- Annealing: furnace cool; normalising: air cool; quenching: martensite; tempering below $A_1$.
- Hardenability is tested by the Jominy test and increased by alloying (except cobalt).
- Austenitic stainless steel: non-magnetic, not hardenable by heat treatment.
- Brass = Cu + Zn; bronze = Cu + Sn; duralumin = Al + Cu + Mg + Mn.
- Thermoplastics re-soften; thermosets (Bakelite) do not.

## 7. Industrial engineering

- EOQ: ordering cost equals holding cost at the optimum.
- Break-even $= \dfrac{F}{p - v}$.
- PERT $t_e = \dfrac{t_o + 4t_m + t_p}{6}$; critical path is the longest path with zero float.
- Transportation basic feasible solution has $m + n - 1$ allocations; VAM gives the best initial solution.
- Control limits at $\pm3\sigma$; c chart for defects per unit; p chart for fraction defective.

## 8. Final-week method

- Day 1 to 3: handbook and checklists, one subject group per day.
- Day 4 to 6: PYQ bank by subject, timed.
- Day 7 and later: full mocks and the error log only.
- Sleep and rest in the last two days; do not start new material.
