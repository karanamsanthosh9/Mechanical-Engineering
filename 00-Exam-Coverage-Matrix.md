## Topics by exam focus

### Common to all three exams (study first)

- Elastic constants, stress-strain curve, thermal stress
- SFD/BMD, bending equation, torsion equation
- Four-bar mechanism, Grashof law, DOF, gear terminology
- Laws of thermodynamics, Otto/Diesel/Brayton/Rankine, entropy
- Fourier law, composite wall, fins, LMTD
- IC engine performance (IP, BP, efficiencies), knocking, octane/cetane
- VCRS COP, refrigerants, psychrometric processes
- Bernoulli, Venturimeter, pipe losses, viscosity, buoyancy
- Pelton/Francis/Kaplan, centrifugal pump, specific speed
- Casting, welding, machining, forming fundamentals
- Heat treatment, Fe-Fe3C diagram, hardness tests

### Mainly APPSC AEE

- Deflection by Macaulay, moment area, Castigliano
- Columns (Euler/Rankine), thick cylinders, failure theories with numericals
- Vibrations (damped, forced, isolation), balancing of multi-cylinder engines
- Machine design: fatigue (Goodman/Soderberg), welded/bolted joint design, bearings, Lewis equation
- Boundary layer, dimensional analysis, compressible flow basics
- Multi-stage compressors, gas turbine modifications, nozzle theory
- Standards and codes asked as MCQs (IS codes, preferred numbers)

### Mainly SSC JE

- Direct formula numericals in SOM, fluid mechanics, thermal
- Metrology, jigs and fixtures, CNC G-codes
- Industrial engineering formulas (EOQ, PERT, break-even)
- Pumps/turbines specific speed ranges

### Mainly RRB JE

- Definitions and one-liners: property classification, boiler mountings, welding types
- Power plant basics (nuclear, hydro, diesel, solar, wind)
- Refrigerants, materials and their uses, basic workshop technology
- Safety, tools, measuring instruments, basic numericals

## Recommended study order

1. Engineering Mechanics, SOM (foundation for TOM and Design)
2. Thermodynamics, then IC Engines, RAC, Power Plant, Heat Transfer
3. Fluid Mechanics, then Fluid Machinery
4. TOM, Machine Design
5. Materials, Manufacturing, Industrial Engineering
6. PYQ bank, revision system
